A FEW PRACTICAL QUESTIONS

Know what
you're getting.

Does this replace our quoting software?

Your team keeps preparing and pricing quotes in its existing tools. AgentWright handles the agreed follow-up process after selected quotes are sent. The initial scope does not include ERP integration, CAD analysis or automated pricing.

Will it send messages without our control?

You approve the wording, timing and eligible quotes. We begin with reviewed drafts. Automatic sending is enabled only after the workflow has been tested in your account and you authorize it. Detected replies to tracked conversations pause reminders. Your team reviews ambiguous or separate-thread responses and records phone orders and other offline decisions in the tracker.

What software do we need?

The first version uses one Google Workspace Gmail account, Google Sheets and Make. These accounts belong to you. Any required software subscriptions are separate from AgentWright's fee. Outlook and custom integrations are outside the initial pilot.

What if we already have follow-up automation?

If it works well, you may not need this service. The pilot is for businesses with a specific gap in their current process. We will check that before agreeing to start.

Do you guarantee more sales?

No. Follow-up can help organize opportunities and obtain responses, but price, timing, fit and buying decisions are outside this system's control. The pilot measures what changes in your actual process.

How much can we customize?

We configure your signature, timing, approved messages and tracker owner. The workflow and supported tools remain consistent. New integrations and custom features are outside this offer. Monitoring and maintenance cover the agreed workflow; the service agreement sets support hours and response expectations.
