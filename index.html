What "an AI team" means

Not one chatbot. A team, each with one job.

Working together the way a good office does: one takes in new work, one keeps track, one looks things up, one drafts, one coordinates and one reports back. Each team is set up around your workflows.

Intake

Picks up new requests wherever they arrive (email, forms, shared folders), sorts them and starts the right workflow.

Tracker

Keeps deadlines, renewals and follow-ups on the calendar and in your tracking sheets, and tells you what's slipping.

Researcher

Gathers what each job needs: past documents, client history, public notices and the details already in your files.

Drafter

Writes first drafts of quotes, letters, replies, outlines and reminders from your own past work and templates.

Coordinator

Proposes meeting times, builds checklists and hands each step to the right assistant or person.

Reporter

Gives the owner a short daily or weekly summary: what needs you, what's waiting on whom, what's overdue.

Every team is different. We build yours around your work, not a template you have to fit into. We run our own business this way every day, and we'll show you a live example on the call.

Example teams

What an AI team can take off your plate.

These are examples of the work we set up, not results from past clients. On the planning call we pick the jobs that matter most to you.

Government service contractors

  • Morning digest of new public notices (for example from SAM.gov) in your codes and set-asides.
  • Response deadlines on your calendar, with reminders.
  • First drafts of capability statements and past-performance write-ups from your own documents.
  • Never logs in to government portals or submits anything. You review and submit.

Food & beverage distributors

  • Order inbox sorted every morning: new orders, changes, price and availability questions, delivery questions, with drafted replies.
  • Unanswered quotes and past-due invoices tracked, with polite reminders drafted.
  • Doesn't enter orders, change prices or promise delivery dates. Your team does.

Property management companies

  • Tenant and owner emails sorted, with urgent maintenance requests flagged first.
  • Lease renewals, move-outs and inspections on the calendar, with reminders.
  • Drafted rent reminders, vendor scheduling emails and owner updates for your team to send.
  • Never screens applicants or makes leasing decisions.

Accounting, bookkeeping and tax firms

  • A running list of which clients still owe which documents, with reminders drafted for your team to send.
  • Engagement letters, booking and filing deadlines tracked.
  • A morning summary for the partners.
  • Admin work only: tax returns and ID numbers stay out of the assistants.

And anyone else

Construction and trade firms, agencies, nonprofits, school admin offices, consultants. If your week is mostly email, calendar and documents, book a call and we'll tell you whether an AI team fits.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Anything else? Email hello@agentwright.ai or ask on the intro call.

Do you only work with certain industries?

No. We build around your workflow, whatever the field. The examples above are just the jobs we see most.

Do you guarantee we'll save time or win more business?

No. An AI team can take a lot of routine sorting and drafting off your people, but how much depends on your work and how you use it. We agree the jobs up front and review what's working at each monthly check-in.

Which AI software do you use?

A leading AI assistant platform that you subscribe to in your own name, starting at about $30/month. It can go up depending on usage and the plan you end up needing. We'll go through it on the intro call, including what it costs and what it can and can't connect to.

Do you need our passwords?

No. You sign in to your own accounts during the setup sessions. We never store your logins.

Will it send emails on its own?

No. Assistants prepare drafts and summaries. Someone on your team approves and sends.

What do we need already?

Google Workspace or Microsoft 365 email and calendar. Shared files help. No new software beyond the AI subscription.

Can it log in to our other systems, submit things or move money?

No. It drafts, tracks and reminds. Submissions, orders, payments and decisions stay with your team.

What if the AI software changes?

It can. Keeping up with those changes is part of the monthly check-in. If it stops being useful, cancel any month.

Can we use it for health data, tax returns or CUI?

No. Those stay out of the assistants.

Do you work outside the US?

Not at the moment. We work with small US businesses and organizations.

Can we cancel?

Yes. The $150/month is month to month, and the AI subscription is yours to keep or cancel.
